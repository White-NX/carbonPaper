# What's NEW / 新增了什么？

## 新功能

### 搜索回顾，找回事情的经过 #241

现在，高级搜索可以同时查找快照文字与每日回顾，也可以切换为“仅快照”或“仅回顾”。除了关键词匹配，你还可以启用回顾语义搜索，按含义补充相关活动。结果会展示匹配线索和语义相关度，并支持按时间与应用筛选，帮助你从零散的屏幕文字找到一件事的完整经过。

回顾语义搜索暂时不可用时，已经找到的关键词结果仍会保留。AI 搜索和外部 MCP 助手也可以搜索保存的回顾，继续查看活动及其来源记录。

Advanced Search can now search captured text and Daily Recaps together, or focus on snapshots or recaps alone. Alongside keyword matching, optional recap semantic search finds activities with related meaning. Results show matching evidence and semantic relevance, with time and application filters to help you follow an activity beyond isolated screen text.

Keyword results remain available when semantic retrieval is temporarily unavailable. AI search and external MCP assistants can also search saved recaps and inspect the activities and source records behind them.

### 从一张快照，回到当天的活动 #241

浏览时间线时，快照详情现在会显示这张记录关联的回顾活动。你可以展开阅读活动描述，查看记录覆盖的时间范围，再直接跳转到当天的回顾继续查看。回顾整理过程也增加了实时进度和阶段提示，等待后台调度时的状态更加清晰。

While browsing the timeline, snapshot details now show associated recap activities. Expand an activity to read its description and see the time range covered by its records, then jump directly to that day's recap. Recap generation also has live progress and stage indicators, including clearer states while waiting for background scheduling.

### 锁定和重启后，回顾也能继续整理 #241

启用后台整理后，在本次运行中完成一次解锁，回顾生成可以在关闭主窗口或重新锁定后继续进行。配合“重启后继续后台整理”，新记录的回顾处理还可以在应用重启、尚未解锁历史记录时进行；较早记录或缺少待处理内容的时段仍会等待解锁。

查看和搜索历史记录、修改回顾及手动重新生成仍需解锁。使用外部模型服务的同意设置与用量限制继续生效，已有的回顾修改和按天删除设置也会保留。

With background organization enabled, recap generation can continue after closing the main window or locking the archive once you have unlocked it during the current run. Combined with the option to continue background processing after a restart, recaps for newly staged records can also be processed before the archive is unlocked again. Older records and periods without the required staged content wait for unlocking.

Browsing and searching history, editing recaps, and manual regeneration still require an unlocked session. Consent for external model services and request budgets remain in effect, as do existing recap corrections and day-deletion preferences.

## 现代化 UI

### 首页和搜索，各自保留浏览位置 #244

首页与高级搜索现在各自拥有时间线。切换页面后，原来的浏览位置和缩放范围会保留，方便你在浏览历史与检查搜索结果之间来回切换。搜索时间线还可以收起为紧凑的结果标记条，收起状态会记住，再次展开即可继续浏览。

Home and Advanced Search now have independent timelines. Switching pages preserves each timeline's position and zoom range, making it easier to move between browsing history and examining search results. The search timeline can also collapse into a compact result-marker strip; its collapsed state is saved, and expanding it restores the full view.

### 智能聚类，列表和详情更好安排 #244

智能聚类的列表宽度现在可以拖动调整，也支持使用键盘调整并记住你选择的宽度。新建、刷新和处理队列等操作集中在列表旁，暂停、继续和删除则放在当前分类的详情中，让浏览和管理分类更加顺手。

The Smart Clusters list can now be resized by dragging or with the keyboard, and your preferred width is saved. Creation, refresh, and queue controls are grouped beside the list, while pause, resume, and deletion actions stay with the selected cluster's details.

## 性能与运行体验

### 更快看到图片，更清楚查看搜索结果 #245

打开快照时，图片现在可以先显示，文字与其他详情随后补齐。时间线也会优先加载当前需要的标签和缩略图，快速切换记录时减少过时任务的影响。

图片搜索的下拉结果和网格会在卡片接近可见区域时加载原图，让画面中的细节更加清楚。已加载的原图会在继续浏览当前结果时保留；清空图片查询后，下拉结果会立即清除。

When opening a snapshot, its image can now appear as soon as it is ready, while text and other details load independently. Timeline labels and thumbnails are loaded in prioritized batches, reducing the impact of outdated work when you move quickly between records.

Picture-search dropdown and grid results load original images as their cards approach the visible area, making visual details easier to inspect. Loaded originals remain available while browsing the current results, and clearing a picture query immediately clears its dropdown results.

### 面向更多历史记录的搜索优化 #235 #238

我们进一步优化了图片搜索和 OCR 文字搜索的数据读取。图片搜索会复用近期的索引更新，并优先读取当前结果页所需的内容；取消或超时的请求会尽快停止后续读取。文字搜索也减少了不必要的历史扫描，以及修改查询和筛选条件时的重复请求，改善较大记录库中的搜索体验。

Image and OCR text search now perform less unnecessary work as archives grow. Image search reuses recent index updates and retrieves detailed content for the current result page; cancelled or expired requests stop further reads promptly. Text search also avoids unnecessary history scans and duplicate requests when queries or filters change.

### 节电策略及时生效 #237

启用节电模式后，在电池供电时启动应用，或拔掉电源后再开启该设置，都会及时暂停记录。接回电源后，应用会按照你的自动启动偏好恢复。节电期间还减少了自动分类、索引和状态检查等后台工作；历史浏览、前台搜索和明确请求的索引操作仍可使用。

With power saving enabled, capture now pauses when the app starts on battery or when the setting is enabled after unplugging. Reconnecting power resumes capture according to your automatic-start preferences. Power saving also reduces background work such as automatic classification, indexing, and status checks, while history browsing, foreground search, and explicitly requested indexing remain available.

## 调整与升级提示

### MCP 回顾查询接口更新 #241

本版将 MCP 工具契约更新至 v3，并新增 `search_recaps`，与已有的 `get_recap_days`、`get_recap_day` 配合查询保存的回顾。直接调用接口的客户端需要将时间范围参数更新为 `start_ms` / `end_ms`（Unix 毫秒，结束边界不包含在范围内），并使用 `screenshot_id` 查询截图详情。截图结果统一返回 `captured_at_ms`，回顾日期保留记录时的时区含义，分页亦加强了在过滤或结果压缩后的连续性。

MCP now uses tool contract v3, with the new `search_recaps` alongside the existing `get_recap_days` and `get_recap_day`. Clients calling the interfaces directly need to update time-range arguments to `start_ms` / `end_ms` (Unix milliseconds, with an exclusive end) and use `screenshot_id` to request snapshot details. Snapshot results consistently expose `captured_at_ms`, recap dates retain their recorded calendar context, and pagination is more reliable after filtering or result compaction.

## 修复

修补了锁定主窗口后，已有删除确认仍可能处于可操作状态，以及严重错误提示下方的对话框仍可能被 Escape 关闭的问题。覆盖界面现在会统一管理焦点和键盘操作。#236

修补了多个窗口同时请求 Windows 身份验证时，可能出现重复提示的问题；自动索引升级和后台读取会等待解锁。#236

修补了取消存储迁移后，进度界面和任务结束状态可能不同步的问题，并让取消失败后的重试更加清晰。#236

修补了节电状态变化与捕获启停时序不一致，可能导致记录没有及时暂停或恢复的问题。#237

修补了快速切换快照时，图片需要等待文字详情才能显示，以及图片搜索结果在继续浏览时反复加载原图的问题。#245

Fixed deletion confirmations remaining interactive after the main window was locked, and Escape dismissing dialogs beneath a fatal error. Covered surfaces now share consistent focus and keyboard handling. #236

Fixed overlapping Windows authentication prompts from multiple windows. Automatic index upgrades and background reads now wait for unlocking. #236

Fixed storage-migration cancellation leaving progress and task completion out of sync, with clearer retry behavior when cancellation fails. #236

Fixed capture not pausing or resuming promptly when power-saving state changed during capture lifecycle operations. #237

Fixed snapshot images waiting for text details before appearing, and repeated original-image loading while browsing picture-search results. #245

This release adds searchable recap memory and improves workspace navigation, bounded search reads, image loading, and power-policy enforcement:

- Added hybrid recap retrieval over corrected activity documents using keyword and semantic indexes, cached pagination, query coverage reporting, and process filtering. Interactive AI search and MCP share authenticated, privacy-filtered recap handlers.
- Added snapshot-level activity associations, cited observation spans, and source membership. Observation spans describe the first and last supporting records and do not measure continuous work duration.
- Extended app-bound processing with independent recap-input, checkpoint, summary, correction, and configuration grants. Protected writes fence dataset, source, privacy, settings, and correction revisions; missing or stale evidence waits for the normal unlocked path.
- Updated MCP to contract v3 with `get_recap_days`, `get_recap_day`, and `search_recaps`, unified `screenshot_id` / `captured_at_ms` references, saved calendar boundaries, and continuation offsets that remain usable after redaction or AI context compaction.
- Cached CLIP tail overlays, filtered lightweight candidates before hydration, and hydrated visible result pages. Added cooperative deadlines throughout database and CNG reads. OCR candidate and page queries now use bounded primary-key lookups instead of history-wide scans.
- Added direct image-path/key reads and an independent read-handle fallback when metadata work holds the resident connection. Existing encrypted image format, authentication, and path/deletion checks remain intact. Timeline headers, details, and thumbnails load in separate cancellable stages.
- Added viewport-driven original-image loading with a two-request concurrency limit and result-card lifetime retention. Home and Search preserve separate timeline cameras; compact timelines avoid screenshot-history queries, and Smart Clusters saves a keyboard-accessible split width.
- Reconciled power policy at startup, settings changes, and polling; applied cancellation and admission checks to capture and automatic processing without spending retry budgets on policy deferrals.
- Shared in-flight Windows Hello verification across windows, coordinated overlay visibility and focus, centralized native file selection, and made migration cancellation settle explicitly.
- Modularized storage and semantic-runtime tests, shared automatic indexing execution, introduced typed background-task failures, and updated Vite to 8.3.5. Added behavioral regressions for search scaling, encryption compatibility, cancellation, authentication, and workspace state.
